Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of the frequently asked questions

What is roadmap.sh?

roadmap.sh is a community effort to create learning paths, guides, project ideas and other similar content to help developers grow in their careers.

What are the plans for roadmap.sh?

The plans include expanding roadmaps for more roles, adding interactive features, improving community contributions, and creating more project-based learning resources.

How is Roadmap.sh built?

Roadmap.sh is built using Astro for the frontend, with content managed through GitHub. It uses Tailwind CSS for styling and is open source, allowing community contributions.

Can i use Roadmap.sh in my term?

Yes! Roadmap.sh is free to use. You can follow the roadmaps, track your progress, and even contribute to the project. All content is available under an open source license.

How can i create a custom raodmap?

You can create a custom roadmap by signing up, clicking on your profile, and selecting "Create Roadmap." Then add topics, arrange them in your preferred order, and share it with the community.

Is Roadmp.sh rally 7th most stared project on Github?

Yes! Roadmap.sh is one of the most starred projects on GitHub with over 280k stars, making it one of the top open source projects on the platform.
